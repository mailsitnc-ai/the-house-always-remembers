# First-time player walkthrough

For someone who knows nothing about the story. You should not need anyone beside you.

**Controls:** W A S D move · mouse look · **E** interact · **F** torch · **J** journal · Esc pause. Headphones help.
Press **Begin**, click the game once to capture the mouse.

## 1. The opening (about a minute, skippable with Space or Enter)
- A photograph of two children, and Daniel (you) thinking aloud: it has been eleven years since his sister Emily died.
- A telephone rings. A child's voice says, "Why did you leave me, Daniel?"
- You stand at the gate of your family's old house. A light is on. Nobody should be there.
- Then you are in control. A line of controls appears for a few seconds.

## 2. Chapter 2, Homecoming
The line at the bottom left always tells you the **one thing to do now**. It brightens for a few seconds when it changes,
and shows a second line saying where to go and how. Press **J** for the full objective at any time. If you stay on one
objective for a minute and a half it brightens again.

1. **"Go into the living room."** Through the doorway on your left as you enter. A soft glimmer marks what the current objective is about.
2. **"Examine the family photograph."** West wall of the living room. Walk up to it. A prompt says `[E] Examine`. Press E and a large view opens. Press E again to put it away.
   *What happens:* Daniel remembers. Ten seconds later you hear footsteps on the floor above.
3. **"Read Mum's note in the kitchen."** Out of the living room, down the hall to its end, east into the kitchen. It is on the worktop by the kettle.
   *What happens:* the first chapter of the story closes and **Chapter 3** begins; a door creaks upstairs and a light dips.

## 3. Chapter 3, The upstairs room
4. **"Find Emily's bedroom."** Take the stairs in the hallway. At the top, Emily's door is the first on your left. Walk in.
   *First entity:* **the Witness** appears across the room, near her desk. See below. It is not dangerous. It never comes close.
5. **"Look at what the figure was watching."** It was looking at Emily's **toy recorder** on her desk. Press E, then play the tape.
   *What happens:* you hear Emily and Daniel arguing. The Witness lowers its head and fades. Chapter 4 begins.

## 4. Chapters 4 to 8, in short
- **Ch 4, Family portraits:** the living room photograph shows five people; the family's own print should show four. Find the
  album in the study (east of the foyer). The study is locked: look for a way in. Reading the print
  begins Chapter 5.
- **Ch 5, The Matron:** a tape appears in the study's tape deck that claims to be Emily. Compare it with her real recordings in
  the journal's puzzle. Somewhere in this chapter a tall woman in a green dress (**the Matron**) first appears at the far end of
  the hall. Stay calm, do not run. Light is the only thing she respects.
- **Ch 6, Eleven years ago:** find what your family packed away, including a locked storage room upstairs (a key is
  somewhere downstairs). A letter from your mother is inside.
- **Ch 7, The missing night:** open the journal (J), Timeline, and put the evening in order.
- **Ch 8, The last door:** the Matron hunts you through the house until you reach Emily's room. What you have understood
  decides which of three endings you can choose.

## 5. When you meet each entity
| Entity | First meeting | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **The Witness** (amber, hat and coat, holds a small photograph) | Chapter 3, Emily's bedroom, by the toy recorder | Look, then take the evidence it guards. It only watches. |
| **The Matron** (tall, green dress) | Chapter 5, the hall, foyer, living or dining room | Do not run. Stay in lit rooms. She speaks half-truths. |
| **The Hollow** (a black, jerking figure; denial) | Chapter 4 onward, only after you have spent a while in a dark living room, dining room, kitchen, study or Daniel's bedroom | Switch on a lamp, or shine your torch (F) on it. It breaks up. |
| **The Unremembered** (pale cut-out children that repeat one movement) | Chapter 3 onward, on the landing or in Emily's room, after the Witness has gone | They only repeat. Look straight at them and they go. |

Lit rooms are your safe places: switch lamps on with E.
